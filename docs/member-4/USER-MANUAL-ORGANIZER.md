# User manual — Campaign organizers (blood banks and donation organizations)

Who can use this: staff of a **verified** blood bank or donation organization. If your organization is still
pending, rejected or suspended you are sent to the account-status page instead.

## Create a campaign
1. Open **Create campaign** (or **New campaign** on My campaigns).
2. Fill in: **Title (English)**, **Title (Urdu)**, **Description (English)**, **Description (Urdu)**, **Date**,
   **Time** (Karachi time), **Venue**, **Area**, **Target blood groups** (All blood groups, or one group) and the
   **Contact phone** (e-mail is optional).
3. Press **Save draft** to keep working later (only a title, a future date and time, a venue and an area are needed),
   or **Submit for approval** to send it to the administrator (the Urdu title, an English description and a contact
   phone are also needed). Red messages under a field tell you what to fix.

## My campaigns
Open **My campaigns**. Each row shows the status:

| Status | Meaning | What you can do |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Draft | Saved, not sent | Edit, Submit, Cancel |
| Pending approval | Waiting for the administrator | Cancel |
| Approved | Visible to donors | Edit, Cancel; once it has started, record outcomes |
| Rejected | The administrator said no (reason is shown) | — (create a new one) |
| Cancelled | You cancelled it | — |
| Completed | The date has passed | Record outcomes |

Press a campaign to open it. The detail page shows the edit form, the **Interested donors** list (name, blood group,
area) and the **Cancel campaign** button.

### Changing an approved campaign
Changing the **date, time, venue, area or description** sends the campaign back to the administrator
("Pending approval"). Donors keep seeing the old approved version until it is approved again. Changing only the title,
contact details or target group takes effect at once.

### Cancel
Press **Cancel campaign**, then confirm. Donors who marked **I plan to attend** are notified.

## Record the outcome (after the drive starts)
1. Open the campaign from **My campaigns** (an approved drive that has started opens this form directly), or press
   **Record outcomes** on its detail page.
2. Enter **Attendees** and **Donations** (donations cannot be more than attendees).
3. To add a donation to a registered donor's history, type the donor's **phone** (for example 0300 1234567) and press
   **Save**. The donor's history gets a **Recorded by org** entry. Repeat for the next donor.
4. Press **Save** without a phone to correct the counts. The campaign becomes **Completed**.

## Problems
* "No registered donor has that mobile number." – check the number; the donor must have registered.
* "A donation for that donor is already recorded for this campaign." – each donor can be recorded once per drive.
* "Donations cannot be fewer than the donors already recorded." – raise the donations number.
