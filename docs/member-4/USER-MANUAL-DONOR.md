# User manual — Donors (profile, history, reminders and campaigns)

Who can use this: **registered donors**. Log in with your mobile number and password. Use the language
button in the header to switch between English and اردو (the page becomes right-to-left).

## My profile
1. Open **Profile** in the menu.
2. Choose your **Blood group** and your **Area** of Karachi.
3. Set **Availability**: Available or Not available.
4. Under **How should we contact you?** tick **SMS**, **WhatsApp** and/or **Platform**. Keep at least one ticked.
5. Under **Notices** tick **Urgent requests** and/or **Campaign notices**.
6. Choose your **Language** and press **Save**. The page changes language straight away.

### Delete my account
Press **Delete account**, then read the warning ("Delete your account? This cannot be undone.") and press the confirm button. Your name and phone are removed,
you are logged out and you cannot log in again. Past donation records stay without your name. This cannot be undone.

## My donation history
1. Open **History**. The title shows how many donations you have in total, for example **Donations (total 4)**.
2. Each row has a date and a label: **Self reported** (you added it) or **Recorded by org** (a blood bank or
   campaign organizer recorded it for you). Newest is first.
3. To add an earlier donation press **+ Add past donation**, choose the **date** (today or earlier) and write the
   **place**, then **Save**.

## Reminder
On the History page, type a number in **Remind me after … days** (1 to 365) and press **Save**. When that many days
have passed since your last donation you will get a reminder in the bell menu (and by SMS if you ticked SMS).
Leave the box empty and save to turn the reminder off.

## Campaigns (blood drives)
1. Open **Campaigns**. You see upcoming drives by **verified** organizers only.
2. Narrow the list with **Area**, **From date** and **Group**, or type in the search box, then press **Search**.
   **Clear filters** shows everything again.
3. Press a drive to see its title, date and time, venue, target blood groups, organizer (with the Verified mark),
   description and contact.
4. Press **I plan to attend** if you intend to go. This is **not a promise** — press **I no longer plan to attend**
   at any time. You can see how many donors plan to attend, and the organizer sees your name, blood group and area
   (not your phone number).
5. If you chose campaign notices, you are told in the bell menu when a drive near you for your blood group is approved,
   and if a drive you marked is cancelled.

## Problems
* "Choose a blood group / area" – pick a value from the list.
* "Keep at least one way of being contacted selected" – tick SMS, WhatsApp or Platform.
* "The date cannot be in the future" – choose today or an earlier day.
* A drive link shows "not found" – the drive was cancelled, is not approved yet, or has finished.
