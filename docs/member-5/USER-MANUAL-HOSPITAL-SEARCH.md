# User manual — Searching for available blood (Hospital)

Who can use this: **staff of a verified hospital**. Donors, blood banks and the
public cannot see blood-bank stock.

## Find blood
1. Log in with your hospital account.
2. Open **Search blood** in the menu.
3. Choose a **Blood group** and a **Component** (Whole blood, Red blood cells, Platelets or Plasma). Both are required.
4. Press **Search**.

## Read the results
Each blood bank that has matching blood shows:
* the bank name with a green **Verified** mark
* **units available** (units on hold for someone else are not counted)
* **Exp** – the earliest expiry date of that blood, the bank's area and its phone (tap to call)
* the bank's e-mail
* **Inventory last updated** – when the bank last changed its stock

Banks with the most units appear first.

> **Important:** availability is reported by the blood bank. Always confirm directly with the blood bank before relying on it.

## What is never shown
Expired blood, blood still awaiting testing or rejected, reserved units, and anything
from a bank that is not verified.

## If nothing is found
You will see "No matching blood is currently reported available". Press
**Create a blood request** to ask for this blood instead (request creation arrives in Sprint 2).

## Problems
* "Choose a blood group." / "Choose a component." – pick a value from the list and search again.
* You are sent to the status page – your hospital is not verified yet; wait for administrator approval.
* Urdu: use the language switch in the header; the page becomes right-to-left.
